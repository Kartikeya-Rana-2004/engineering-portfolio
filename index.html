CivicFlow: Resilient Ingestion & Medallion Pipeline

Medallion ETL

Extracts operational data, writes immutable Bronze raw batches, validates schemas, executes idempotent Silver upserts, and computes Gold daily resolution metrics.

1. Bronze Landing

Raw batch JSONL stored with SHA-256 digest and explicit time-boundary watermark.

2. Quality Gate

ContractWatch inspection; batches with >5% errors quarantined atomically.

3. Silver Keyed Upsert

Content-hash comparison prevents duplicate disk writes; updates status in place.

4. Gold Aggregate View

Aggregated daily SLA metrics by agency, borough, and resolution timeframe.
