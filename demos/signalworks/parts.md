# Figure: event inspection and receipt machine
Mechanism: a red parcel moves from an infeed through an inspection gate to a committed receipt dock, a replay-return dock, or a quarantine tray. A rolled-back parcel stays at inspection. These are recorded transaction outcomes, not live computation.

Purposeful solids: 4 feet + base plate (5); 4 mounting pads + 4 bolts (13); conveyor bed + 12 transverse tread plates + 2 guide rails (28); 4 inspection posts + lintel + sensor head + sensor aperture (35); 4 post shoes (39); receipt pedestal + press body + press slide + stamp + output card (44); quarantine tray floor + 4 rims (49); return dock floor + 2 rims (52); 4 rail end stops (56); control plinth + switch base + switch cap (59); parcel + parcel seal (61). All structure mounts to the base/bed. Parcel translates over the conveyor or designated tray/dock only.

Use only rectilinear slabs: no round parts, tubes, or pipes. Geometry audit requirements for those shapes do not apply. Painter's order uses world depth, with supports below their children and parcel inserted into its physical depth slot. Line checks cover both themes and parcel resting poses.
